# Winning product research – October 5, 2026

## Pick: Rechargeable magnetic 2-in-1 hand warmer

**Sell price:** $34.99 (1 set) · $59.99 (2 sets) · $99.99 (4-set gift pack)
**Supplier cost:** about $5–9 + $3–6 shipping
**Gross profit per 1-set order:** about $20 → **break-even ad cost per sale ≈ $20**
(2-set orders ≈ $31 profit, 4-set ≈ $50, so pushing bundles is how you win)

### Why this product, right now
| Criteria | Hand warmer |
|---|---|
| **Timing** | Cold season is starting in the US, Canada, UK and Europe. It sells from **October through February**, so you have 5 months, not 3 weeks like Halloween products |
| **Christmas** | A stocking-stuffer gift under $40 for anyone: dads, commuters, hunters, skiers, students. The 4-pack is a "whole family" gift |
| **Easy to show in a video** | Split it, snap it, show the heat (thermal camera, frosty window, ice cube melting). Shows clearly what it does in 3 seconds |
| **Margin** | 3–5× markup on product cost |
| **Who buys it** | Men and women, 18–65, so the Facebook audience is huge |
| **Easy for ads and returns** | No sizing (few returns), nothing fragile, no medical claims needed (easy Meta approval) |
| **Data** | Electric hand warmer market is about $610M (2025) and projected to reach about $958M by 2033 (Sourcinbox); it is on multiple October 2026 trend lists |

### What I rejected and why
| Product | Why not |
|---|---|
| Halloween items (LED masks, bats, eyeball doorbell) | Halloween is in 26 days. With 7–15 day shipping, the selling window is basically gone |
| Heated jacket | Sizing means returns. Product cost is $25+, which leaves a thin margin after ads |
| Portable nebulizer | Medical device: regulated, and Meta restricts the ads |
| Heated period belt (my first pick) | Ads are mostly to women only, and Meta is strict about health-related wording. Still a decent backup for a women-focused store |
| Squishy toys, pencil cases | The order value is too low for paid Facebook ads to be profitable |

### Backup products to test if the hand warmer doesn't work
1. **Rechargeable heated lunch box** ($30–40 sell, $6–14 cost). For commuters and workers, and it works as a gift.
2. **Electric salt & pepper grinder set** ($30–40 sell, $7–9 cost). Holiday cooking and gifting.
3. **Heated period belt** (the store from earlier in git history can be restored).

### Honest note
No one can guarantee a winner. Trend lists and market data tell you what *can* work. **Your ad test tells you
what *does* work.** I don't have access to paid ad-spy tools (Minea, PiPiADS etc.), so before you launch, search
"hand warmer rechargeable" in the free **Facebook Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library) and look at which ads
have been running for 2+ weeks. That means they're profitable. Copy the *angle*, never the video.

### Where it's sold (launch markets)
- **Start with: United States + Canada.** Cold winter, big market, and no import VAT registration needed for these orders.
- **Later: UK and EU.** The UK requires you to register for and collect VAT on orders up to £135. The EU needs IOSS
  registration. Doable, but set it up before turning those markets on.
- **Skip: Australia / South America.** It's summer there.
