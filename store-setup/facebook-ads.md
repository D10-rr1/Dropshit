# Facebook / Instagram ads – Emberwell hand warmer

## $300 budget plan

Don't spend it all in one night. Meta needs a few days to learn who buys, and new ad accounts usually have a daily
spending limit anyway.

| Day | Spend | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | ~$50/day | Test 5 creatives (below) in 1 campaign |
| 4 | – | Turn off losers (rules below) |
| 4–6 | ~$50/day | Spend on the 1–2 winners, add 2 new versions of the winner |

### Campaign setup
- **Objective:** Sales → conversion event **Purchase**
- **Budget:** ABO (ad set budget), **1 ad set per creative**, ~$10/day each
- **Countries:** United States + Canada (see WINNING-PRODUCT.md for why)
- **Audience:** All genders, 25–65, no interests (broad works best on a new pixel)
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements

### Kill and keep rules (after ~$15–20 spent per ad)
| Metric | Kill if | Good if |
|---|---|---|
| Link CTR | < 1 % | > 1.5 % |
| Link CPC | > $1.20 | < $0.70 |
| Add to cart | 0 after $20 | ≥ 2 |
| Cost per purchase | > $20 (break-even) | < $14 |

If **nothing** works after ~$150, the problem is usually the creative or the offer, not the targeting. Make new hooks
before switching product.

### Meta ad rules
- ❌ Don't say "cures arthritis / Raynaud's / poor circulation" (health claims get rejected)
- ❌ Don't use fake news-style headlines or fake "sold out" claims
- ✅ Talk about **warm hands, cold mornings, gifts, ditching disposable warmers**

## 5 video scripts (UGC style, 15–25 sec, vertical 9:16, text on screen)

Order 2 samples from your supplier and film these yourself on your phone. Self-filmed UGC beats supplier videos every
time. Put the hook text on screen in the **first 2 seconds**.

**1. "The snap"**
- 0–2s: Close-up: pull the two halves apart, snap them back. Text: *"Wait… it splits in two?"*
- 2–8s: Hand one half to a friend or partner. Text: *"One for me, one for you"*
- 8–15s: Show the button and 3 heat levels + USB-C charging
- CTA: *"Free shipping today 👉"*

**2. "Disposable warmers are a scam"**
- 0–2s: Throw a pile of used disposable warmer packets in the trash. Text: *"I spent $60 on these last winter 🤦"*
- 2–10s: Pull out Emberwell, turn it on. *"Now I charge this like my phone"*
- 10–18s: Outside in the cold, hands in pockets, smiling
- CTA: *"Rechargeable. Use it all winter."*

**3. "Ice test"**
- 0–2s: Place it next to an ice cube on a plate. Text: *"How hot does it actually get?"*
- 2–12s: Timelapse of the ice melting
- 12–18s: Hold it in your hands outside. *"3 heat levels, pocket size"*

**4. "Gift for dad"**
- 0–2s: Text *"The only gift my dad actually used every day"*
- 2–12s: Dad (or anyone) at a football game, fishing, scraping ice off a car, using it
- 12–20s: Gift box close-up. *"4-pack = Christmas shopping done"*

**5. "POV: your hands are always cold"**
- 0–2s: Shivering at a bus stop / dog walk. Text: *"POV: your hands are ALWAYS cold"*
- 2–10s: Slide each half into each glove or pocket
- 10–15s: Relief face. CTA: *"Warm hands, anywhere"*
