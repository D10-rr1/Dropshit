# Facebook / Instagram ads – Värmly

## Budget plan for $300 (≈ 3 100 kr)

Don't spend it all in one night. Meta needs a few days to learn, and new ad accounts usually have a daily
spending limit anyway.

| Day | Spend | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | ~$50/day | Test 5 creatives (below) in 1 campaign |
| 4 | – | Turn off losers (rules below) |
| 4–6 | ~$50/day | Spend on the 1–2 winners, add 2 new versions of the winner |

### Campaign setup
- **Objective:** Sales → conversion event **Purchase**
- **Budget:** ABO (ad set budget), **1 ad set per creative**, ~$10/day each
- **Audience:** Sweden, women 18–45 (also test 18–55 broad), no interests (broad works best on a new pixel)
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements
- **Language of ads:** Swedish

### Kill and keep rules (after ~$15–20 spent per ad)
| Metric | Kill if | Good if |
|---|---|---|
| CTR (link) | < 1 % | > 1.5 % |
| CPC (link) | > 12 kr | < 7 kr |
| Add to cart | 0 after $20 | ≥ 2 |
| Cost per purchase | > 230 kr (break-even) | < 150 kr |

If **no** creative works after ~$150, the problem is usually the creative or the offer, not the targeting. Make new hooks
before you consider a new product.

## Meta ad rules for this product
- ❌ "Botar mensvärk", "smärtlindring", "medicinsk", "kliniskt bevisad": health claims get rejected (and break EU rules)
- ❌ Before/after, "Är du trött på din mens?" (Meta doesn't allow ads that point at the viewer's personal health)
- ✅ Talk about **warmth, comfort, cozy, cold days, freedom from the hot water bottle**

## 5 video scripts (UGC style, 15–25 sec, vertical 9:16, Swedish text on screen)

Film them yourself with the sample, or order UGC from Swedish creators (e.g. on Fiverr or Insense). Put the hook text on
screen in the **first 2 seconds**.

**1. "Varmvattenflaskan"**
- 0–2s: Hold a hot water bottle, throw it on the sofa. Text: *"Jag har slutat med varmvattenflaska 🙅‍♀️"*
- 2–8s: Put on the belt, press the button. Text: *"Trådlöst. Varmt på sekunder."*
- 8–15s: Walk around, make coffee, sit at the desk. Text: *"…och jag kan röra mig"*
- 15–20s: Close-up of the 3 levels + USB-C. CTA: *"Fri frakt idag 👉"*

**2. "Under tröjan"**
- 0–2s: Text *"Ingen på kontoret vet att jag har det här på mig"* + person at work, sweater on
- 2–6s: Lift the sweater a bit, show the belt
- 6–15s: Show how thin it is, the button, the light. *"3 värmenivåer, laddas med USB-C"*
- CTA: *"Värmly – länk i annonsen"*

**3. "Kall höstmorgon"**
- 0–2s: Rain or cold outside the window. Text: *"Oktober i Sverige be like 🥶"*
- 2–10s: Put on the belt under the jacket, go out for a walk, smile
- 10–15s: *"Skön värme runt magen eller ländryggen – var du än är"*
- CTA: *"30 dagars öppet köp"*

**4. "Unboxing"**
- 0–2s: Text *"Det här är det bästa jag köpt i höst"* + package
- 2–12s: Unbox, show the belt, cable, button
- 12–20s: Wear it on the sofa with tea and a blanket. *"Mysfaktor 100"*

**5. "3 skäl"**
- Text: *"3 skäl till varför alla frågar om mitt värmebälte"*
- 1) *"Inga sladdar"* 2) *"Varmt på sekunder"* 3) *"Går att ha under kläderna"*
- CTA: *"Köp 2, spara 22 % – perfekt present"*
