Do you love your dog or cat — but not the fur on everything you own?

Pet hair gets into everything. Dogs and cats shed all year, and the hair weaves itself into fabric, where sticky rollers and vacuums barely reach it.

Sticky sheets fill up after one cushion. You throw them away, buy more, and the sofa still isn't clean.

Our solution

Furaway uses a special fabric that grips hair when you roll it one way, and a chamber that collects it as you roll back.

No glue, no batteries, nothing to throw away. Just roll, empty and repeat for years.

How it works

1. Roll back and forth

Short strokes let the fabric lift fur out of the sofa, bed or car seat.

2. Fur goes inside

Each pass sweeps the fur into the built-in chamber, so the roller stays clean.

3. Empty and repeat

Press the release and tip the fur into the bin. Ready for next time.
